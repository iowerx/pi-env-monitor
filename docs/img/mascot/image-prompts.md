# Image Prompts: Mecha the Clockwork Owl

I am about to ask you to generate seven different poses for a book mascot.
Please use a consistent drawing style for all seven images.

See [`character-sheet.md`](character-sheet.md) for the canonical identity
this mascot must match. Each prompt below is fully self-contained — use any
one independently. If your image tool accepts a reference image, attach
`neutral.png` from this directory to keep the design consistent.

The PNGs currently in this directory were drawn programmatically by
`scripts/generate-mecha-mascot.py`. Use these prompts only if you want to
replace them with AI-generated artwork; afterwards, trim the transparent
padding from each image before use.

## 1. Neutral / Default Pose

Please generate a new pose for Mecha the Clockwork Owl. A modern flat vector
illustration of Mecha the Clockwork Owl, a friendly pedagogical mascot for the
Raspberry Pi Environmental Monitoring: Grades 6–12 textbook, a hands-on guide
to building a Raspberry Pi station that measures temperature, pressure, and
humidity and reports the data from the field. Mecha is a compact, upright
mechanical owl automaton with a polished gold/brass (#F2B632) head, wings,
legs, and tail, and a silver (#C9D0D8) chest of overlapping scalloped metal
feathers with an occasional gold feather mixed in. Mecha has two very large
round silver lens eyes set in gold rims, each with a steel aperture ring of
fine clockwork spokes, a white eye, and a warm red-orange iris with white
highlights; a pointed gold beak; layered gold scalloped crown feathers; small
soft ear tufts; gold feather brows; soft pink blush under the eyes; segmented
gold legs with dark banded joints and steel-tipped talons. On Mecha's chest is
a round indigo (#3F51B5) sensor badge in a gold ring with a glowing orange
(#FF9800) LED at its center. Mecha's expression is wide-eyed, curious, and
friendly — never menacing. The character has a big head and eyes relative to
the body and reads well at icon size. Style: modern flat vector illustration,
clean bold dark outlines, soft gradient shading, transparent background with
alpha channel, suitable for embedding in educational content. No text in
image.

Mecha stands upright in a relaxed, neutral pose facing the viewer directly,
with a calm and friendly expression and the beak closed. Both wings rest
folded naturally at their sides with no specific gesture. The pose is balanced
