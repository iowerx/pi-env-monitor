# References: From Measurement to Consequence

1. [Weather forecasting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weather_forecasting) - Wikipedia - Traces weather forecasting from folklore to computer models and explains how observations feed every forecast. Its sections on warnings, aviation, agriculture, and energy match the decisions this chapter connects to measurement.

2. [Urban heat island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urban_heat_island) - Wikipedia - Explains why cities are warmer than the countryside around them, with causes, health effects, and ways to cool them. Its temperature maps show how station data reveals heat islands students can study locally.

3. [Citizen science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citizen_science) - Wikipedia - Describes how volunteers collect and share data that scientists use, with examples from weather, birds, and astronomy. It shows students how their own station could contribute to real research.

4. The Discovery of Global Warming - Spencer R. Weart - Harvard University Press - Weart tells climate science as a story of patient measurement, showing how scattered records, like the Keeling curve, slowly built a convincing case. His approach shows why long, careful climate records matter.

5. The Weather Machine - Andrew Blum - Ecco - Blum follows a forecast backward through the worldwide network of weather stations, satellites, and supercomputers that produces it. His journey-style reporting helps students see how one station's measurements join the global forecasting system.

6. [CoCoRaHS](https://www.cocorahs.org/) - Community Collaborative Rain, Hail and Snow Network - A volunteer network of backyard observers of all ages who measure and map rain, hail, and snow. Its daily data help with flood, drought, and agricultural decisions, and students can join.

7. [The GLOBE Program](https://www.globe.gov/) - GLOBE Program - An international science and education program where students and the public collect environmental data and share it worldwide. Its protocols and data maps show how a school station can contribute to real science.

8. [AQI Basics](https://www.airnow.gov/aqi/aqi-basics/) - AirNow - Explains the Air Quality Index, its color-coded levels, and what each means for health and outdoor activity. It connects the chapter's air quality section to the daily alerts that schools and families use.

9. [U.S. Tsunami Warning Centers](https://www.tsunami.gov/) - NOAA Tsunami Warning Centers - The official site of the U.S. tsunami warning centers, showing current alerts and explaining warning criteria. It shows how seismic and ocean measurements become public warnings within minutes of an earthquake.

10. [Climate Change: Global Temperature](https://www.climate.gov/news-features/understanding-climate/climate-change-global-temperature) - NOAA Climate.gov - Summarizes the global temperature record since 1850, with graphs of yearly temperatures and warming rates. It shows how long instrumental records reveal climate change that no single year could.
