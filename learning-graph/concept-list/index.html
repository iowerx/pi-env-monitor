    Concept List¶

    This is the enumerated concept list for Raspberry Pi Environmental Monitoring: Grades 6-12, generated from course-description.md by learning-graph-generator v0.05.

    There are 269 concepts. Each label is in Title Case, is 32 characters or fewer, and names an entity rather than asking a question. The number is the ConceptID used in learning-graph.csv and learning-graph.json.

    The concepts are listed in taxonomy groups for readability. The grouping matches concept-taxonomy.md. ConceptIDs are stable and must not be renumbered.
